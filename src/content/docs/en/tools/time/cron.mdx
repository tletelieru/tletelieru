Paste a cron expression to read it in plain words and see when it fires next. Parsing uses [croner](https://github.com/Hexagon/croner) and descriptions come from [cronstrue](https://github.com/bradymholt/cRonstrue).

## Format

| Field        | Values                                  |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| seconds      | `0-59` (only with 6 fields)             |
| minute       | `0-59`                                  |
| hour         | `0-23`                                  |
| day of month | `1-31`, `L`                             |
| month        | `1-12` or `JAN-DEC`                     |
| day of week  | `0-7` (0 and 7 are Sunday) or `SUN-SAT` |

Lists (`1,15`), ranges (`9-17`), steps (`*/15`) and nicknames such as `@daily` or `@hourly` are supported.

## Options

- **Time zone**: next runs are computed and shown in this zone (America/Santiago by default), including daylight-saving changes.
- **Number of runs**: 1 to 50.
- **Recalculate from now**: next runs start at the moment the page loaded or the expression changed; use the button to refresh them.

## Examples

| Expression          | Meaning                                                          |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `0 9 * * 1-5`       | At 09:00, Monday through Friday                                  |
| `*/15 9-18 * * 1-5` | Every 15 minutes, between 09:00 and 18:59, Monday through Friday |
| `0 0 1 * *`         | At 00:00, on day 1 of the month                                  |
| `30 0 * * * *`      | At 30 seconds past the minute, at 0 minutes past the hour        |
