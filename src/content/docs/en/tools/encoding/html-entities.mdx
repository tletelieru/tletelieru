Paste text to escape it for HTML, or switch to **Decode** to turn entities back into characters.

<ToolEmbed slug="html-entities" />

## Modes

- **Encode**: escapes `&`, `<`, `>`, `"` and `'` so the text is safe inside HTML.
- **Decode**: resolves named (`&eacute;`), decimal (`&#233;`) and hex (`&#xE9;`) references.

## Encode options

- **Only `& < > " '`**: the minimal set, enough for element content and quoted attributes.
- **Also non-ASCII**: every character above ASCII becomes an entity too (emoji included).
- **Named**: uses names such as `&copy;`, `&mdash;` or `&euro;` when they exist, decimal otherwise.
- **Decimal** / **Hex**: always numeric references, e.g. `&#128512;` or `&#x1F600;`.

## Decode warnings

Unknown entities are kept as they are and listed with their line and column. Legacy names such
as `&copy` or `&amp` and numeric references are still decoded without the trailing `;`, with a
warning. Decoding runs once, so `&amp;amp;` becomes `&amp;`.

## Examples

| Input            | Mode                      | Result                     |
| ---------------- | ------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| `<b>Tom & Jerry` | Encode                    | `&lt;b&gt;Tom &amp; Jerry` |
| `café 😀`        | Encode (non-ASCII, named) | `caf&eacute; &#128512;`    |
| `&#xE9;&hellip;` | Decode                    | `é…`                       |
