## Modes

- **Parse**: reads the EMV TLV fields and checks the CRC16-CCITT in tag 63.
- **Generate**: builds a static BR Code from a key, receiver and city. Name and city lose accents and are cut to 25 and 15 characters, as the standard asks.
- **Validate key**: CPF and CNPJ (with check digits, including alphanumeric CNPJ), email, `+55` phone and random EVP keys.

A generated code follows the Banco Central do Brasil format, but only a real Pix app or PSP confirms that a key exists. Dynamic charges are parsed but not generated.
