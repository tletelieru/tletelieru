## Notes

- An annual rate is treated as nominal and divided by 12.
- The last installment absorbs the rounding, so the balance ends at exactly zero.
- **CAE-style annual cost** is the monthly IRR of the cash flows, net of the upfront fee, multiplied by 12. **Effective annual cost** compounds that IRR.

This is an estimate for comparing offers. The official CAE of a Chilean loan also includes insurance and other costs under its own regulation, so it can differ from this figure.
