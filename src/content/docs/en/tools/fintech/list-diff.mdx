<ToolEmbed slug="list-diff" />

## Notes

- Columns can be separated by comma, semicolon, tab or spaces.
- Amounts are compared as exact decimals, so `10.10` equals `10.1`, and `1.234,50` works too.
- A first row without a valid amount is treated as a header.
- Repeated IDs are summed and listed.
- With amounts off, the whole line is the ID.
