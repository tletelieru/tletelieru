## How it computes

- **Client buys** the base currency: effective rate = mid × (1 + spread) and the client pays gross + fee.
- **Client sells** the base currency: effective rate = mid × (1 − spread) and the client receives gross − fee.
- Spread cost is the difference against the mid rate.

Amounts round half up to the quote currency's decimals (`CLP` 0, `USD` 2). Inverse rates show 8 decimals. All math is exact decimal.
