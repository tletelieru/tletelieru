## Options

- **Weights**: comma-separated. They repeat when the number is longer than the list.
- **Direction**: start from the rightmost or leftmost digit.
- **Result**: `11 − remainder`, or the remainder itself.
- **If 10 / If 11**: the symbol for those results, like `K` and `0` in a Chilean RUT.

The **RUT**, **CPF** and **ISBN-10** buttons fill in those rules. For CPF they compute the first digit; to get the second, append it and use weights starting at 11.
