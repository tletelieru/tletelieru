<ToolEmbed slug="blnk-payload" />

## Notes

- `amount` is written exactly as typed, with no float rounding.
- `precision` turns the amount into the integer Blnk stores: `750.25` at `100` is `75025`. You get a warning if the amount does not fit.
- **Generate reference** creates a random `ref_` value only when you click it. Each transaction needs a unique reference.
- `description` and an empty `meta_data` are left out.
