## Notes

- Amounts and dates are decoded in `:32A:`, `:33B:`, balances (`:60:`, `:62:`, `:64:`, `:65:`) and `:61:` statement lines.
- SWIFT's comma decimals become dots: `1234,56` → `1234.56`. Two-digit years are read as 20YY.
- Other MT types still split into fields, with labels only for the tags they share.
- It reads the structure, but does not validate network rules or field formats.
