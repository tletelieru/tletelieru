## How it computes

- **Factura**: IVA = net × rate, rounded half up, and total = net + IVA.
- **Boleta**: net = total ÷ (1 + rate), rounded half up, and IVA = total − net, so the parts always add up.
- The **Total** row recomputes IVA on the summed base, as a document with several lines does. It can differ by a peso from adding the rows.

`1.190.000` reads as es-CL. A value like `1.000` is ambiguous and is read as one thousand, with a warning.
