## Schemes

- **Stripe**: `Stripe-Signature` with `t=` and `v1=`. Signs `timestamp.body` with the full `whsec_` secret.
- **GitHub**: `X-Hub-Signature-256` with `sha256=`. Signs the body.
- **Svix**: `svix-id`, `svix-timestamp` and `svix-signature`. Signs `id.timestamp.body` with the base64 part of the secret.
- **Generic**: HMAC of the body in SHA-256, SHA-1 or SHA-512, in hex or base64.

Paste the **raw** body: reformatting the JSON changes the signature. The tolerance check uses your clock and only runs in the browser.

Secrets and payloads stay in this tab. They are not written to the URL, history or snippets, and nothing is sent anywhere.
