Ledgers and payment APIs store money as integers in the currency's minor unit: `1234.56` USD is `123456`, `1500` CLP stays `1500` and `1.234` KWD is `1234`. Paste one value per line; the math runs on `BigInt`, never on binary floats, so `1.15` becomes `115` and not `114`.

<ToolEmbed slug="minor-units" />

## Options

- **Direction**: amount to minor units, or back.
- **Currency**: any ISO 4217 currency with defined decimals. The decimals come from the [ISO 4217 table](/tletelieru/en/tools/money/iso-4217/).
- **Extra decimals**: what to do with `1.005` USD. **Reject** flags the line; the other modes round it.

Amounts use a dot for decimals and no thousands separator. Use [number formats](/tletelieru/en/tools/money/number-locale/) to read `1.234,56` first.
