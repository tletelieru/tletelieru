**Format** turns `1234.56` into `1.234,56` (es-CL), `1,234.56` (en-US) or `R$ 1.234,56` (pt-BR). **Parse** does the reverse and returns a plain decimal, ready for code or a database.

<ToolEmbed slug="number-locale" />

## Notes

- Values reach `Intl.NumberFormat` as exact decimal strings, so digits beyond 15 are kept.
- Percent multiplies by 100: `0.1234` shows as `12,34 %`.
- Parsing accepts currency symbols, codes and `%`, but not compact forms such as `1,2 M`.
- Results depend on your browser's locale data.
