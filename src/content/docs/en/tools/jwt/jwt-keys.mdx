Convert keys in both directions, or create a pair for tests. Nothing leaves this tab, and keys are
not added to the URL, history or snippets.

## Convert

Paste a PEM key, a JWK or a JWKS. The tool shows both forms and, for a private key, its public half.

- **Reads**: `PUBLIC KEY`, `PRIVATE KEY`, `RSA PUBLIC KEY`, `RSA PRIVATE KEY` and `EC PRIVATE KEY`.
- **Writes**: SPKI for public keys and PKCS#8 for private ones.
- **Curves**: P-256, P-384 and P-521.

Encrypted PEM files, certificates and Ed25519 keys are reported as unsupported. Symmetric `oct` keys
have no PEM form.

## Generate

Creates RSA 2048/3072/4096, EC P-256/384/521 or HMAC 256/384/512 keys with Web Crypto when you press
the button. **They are for testing only**: for production, generate keys in your secrets manager or
HSM. RSA 4096 can take a few seconds.
