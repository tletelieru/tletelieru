Paste a token, and optionally its key, to get a list of risks sorted by severity. These are
heuristics that help a review; the signature is not checked here. Token and key stay in this tab.

## What it looks for

- **Algorithm**: `alg: none`, a missing or unknown `alg`.
- **Algorithm and key**: a public key offered for an HS token (alg confusion), a secret for RS, PS or
  ES, and a reminder to pin the algorithm for HS tokens.
- **Key strength**: HS keys shorter than the hash and RSA keys under 2048 bits.
- **Lifetime**: no `exp`, more than 30 days, `exp` before `iat`, `nbf` after `exp`, `iat` in the
  future, non-numeric dates.
- **Sensitive claims**: names such as `password`, `secret` or `card`.
- **Risky headers**: `jku` and `x5u` (keys from a URL the token picks), `jwk` and `x5c` (the token
  brings its own key), `crit`, and a `kid` that looks like a path traversal, an injection or a URL.
