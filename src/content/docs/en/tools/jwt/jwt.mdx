Paste a token. A leading `Bearer ` (or `Authorization: Bearer `), spaces and line breaks are
ignored. Decoding happens entirely in your browser: the token is never sent anywhere and is not
stored in the URL, the local history or snippets.

<ToolEmbed slug="jwt" />

## What you get

- **Token view** with the header, payload and signature color-coded.
- **Header** and **payload** as pretty-printed JSON, plus the raw **signature** (base64url) and
  the `alg`.
- **Time claims**: `exp`, `nbf` and `iat` in UTC, in the selected zone (default
  `America/Santiago`) and relative to now ("expires in 12 minutes", "3 hours ago").
- **Status**: valid, expired or not yet valid.

## Warnings

- `alg: none` (unsigned token) or a missing `alg`.
- No `exp`, or a lifetime longer than 30 days.
- `exp` / `nbf` / `iat` that are not NumericDate (seconds since 1970).
- Payload keys that look sensitive (`password`, `secret`, `ssn`, `card`, …): a JWT payload is
  only encoded, anyone holding the token can read it.

## Signature

This tool **does not verify the signature**. A token that decodes is not a trusted token; verify
it with the issuer's key on the server.

Encrypted tokens (JWE, five parts) cannot be decoded without the key and are reported as such.
